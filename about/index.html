About Honest Switch

One person, one rule: never state a price we have not read on the vendor's own page, and never hide how this site makes money.

Who writes this

Honest Switch is written and maintained by Nypsus. It is not a company: there is no editorial team, no advertising department, no investor to please. The pages are static files, published openly, and every claim on them links to its source.

If you find something wrong, write to the address at the bottom of every page. Corrections are made and dated, including when the correction goes against us.

How we make money

This site is not paid to rank anything. The comparison pages list vendors in the order the evidence supports, and no vendor has ever paid for a position here. There are no ads and no sponsored placements.

Affiliate links started on 1 October 2026: a commission is paid by the vendor when a reader subscribes through a labelled link. Each one is labelled on the spot, and the first tool to carry one — GetMyInvoices — was listed on this site before it paid us anything. You can always go to the vendor directly: the same price applies.

What will never happen here: paid rankings, sponsored reviews, invented testimonials, or a “verified” badge on something we did not check.

How prices and facts are checked

Every figure is read on the vendor's official pricing page or official announcement, then linked in the Sources section of the page with its check date. Prices move often — the date matters more than the number, so each page says when it was last checked.

When something cannot be verified (a promotional price that only appears in an ad, a figure we cannot reproduce), it is written as unverified instead of estimated. A missing figure is better than a wrong one.

What we measure — and what we never store

No cookies. No tracking pixels. No third-party analytics, no advertising network, no social widgets. Your browser is not identified.

We do count visits, with our own counter, because knowing whether a page is read at all is the only way to know if it deserves to exist. That counter stores no cookie and no identifier: your IP address is hashed with a daily salt and never kept in clear, so a visit cannot be linked to you, or even to your visit yesterday. We count page views and, on some links, whether they were clicked. That is the whole extent of it.
