Here’s what to check before you jump.

It is — so we compared the client-portal suites people actually move to, with prices we verified on their own pages on 1 October 2026, and the catches nobody mentions.

Before you switch: the checklist

  1. Renewal date first. HoneyBook price increases usually bite at renewal. If you are mid-year on an annual plan, you have time — decide deliberately, not under a deadline.
  2. What’s actually in your workflow? List the flows you run weekly: proposal, contract signature, invoice + payment, appointment scheduling, client messages. The tool that covers all five beats the one with the prettiest portal.
  3. Client portal = where the client clicks. Test with a real test client: does your client see their contracts, invoices and payments in one private place, or is it email + links again?
  4. Payment handling. Check processing fees, currencies, and whether payments land in your own Stripe/PayPal or only inside the platform.
  5. Integrations you already pay for. QuickBooks/Xero sync, Calendly, Google Drive/Notion — verify each on the trial, not on the marketing page.
  6. Export before you cancel. Confirm each candidate imports your clients, projects, invoices and templates; export from HoneyBook first.
  7. Annual vs monthly math. Annual saves roughly 20–40% on every tool above (Bonsai: 2 months of savings; 17hats: 50% first-year promo at check time) — but only commit annually after a month on the tool.

Questions

Did HoneyBook really raise prices?

Yes — the CEO letter of 20 February 2026 announces the first price increase in company history. It publishes no amounts; the current plan prices are on the pricing page (checked above, 1 Oct 2026).

What is a client portal, and do I need one?

A private space where a client sees proposals, contracts, invoices, payments and messages in one place. If you chase contracts and invoices by email today, a portal removes most of the follow-up.

Can I move my client data out of HoneyBook?

Yes — export clients, projects, invoices and templates, and check each candidate’s import options during its free trial before you cancel.

Is there a free tier among these tools?

Bonsai has a free plan (limited clients) and 17hats a free trial; Dubsado and HoneyBook offer free trials. Test your exact daily flow on whichever you shortlist.
