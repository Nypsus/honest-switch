Coaches, trainers, community builders: which platform should your paid community live on?

Creators keep asking the same question: Skool or Circle? — the platform r/CircleSoPlatform thread we start from compares the two in detail. But the real decision has four players, not two: Skool, Circle, Mighty Networks and Kajabi all sell the same promise — a home for your paid community, your courses and your members. Their prices, feature ceilings and payout programs differ a lot. We compared the four with prices read on the vendors’ own pages on 1 October 2026, and the catches the marketing pages do not mention.

In brief

  • Skool: 9 $/mo Hobby · 99 $/mo Pro — flat rate, member count not billed.
  • Circle: from 89 $/mo (Professional) — member-based pricing higher up; the white-label tier has no public price.
  • Mighty Networks: from 79 $/mo (Launch) — growth tiers jump quickly.
  • Kajabi: from 143 $/mo on annual billing — the most expensive of the four.
  • Referral money: Skool 40% recurring; Circle $100 + up to 20% recurring (official pages).

Before you commit: the checklist

1 · The mathsflat or per-member? model your member count at 3× growth, not today’s number.
2 · The scopecourse, community, or both? — it decides between Skool, Circle, Mighty and Kajabi.
3 · The lock-inemail is the real lock-in: one bill or two decides more than the sticker price.
  1. Price per member or flat? Skool and Kajabi charge a flat platform fee; Circle — like Mighty Networks above their entry tier — scales with members or products. Model your headcount at 3× growth, not today’s number.
  2. Courses, community or both? Skool lets a community run one course simply; Circle handles multiple courses with full control; Mighty Networks and Kajabi bundle courses + community + events. If your offer is mostly a course with a side chat, you may be overpaying for community infrastructure.
  3. Email is the real lock-in. Skool has no built-in email; you will bolt on an email tool (and pay for a second subscription). Circle has native email + automation; Kajabi includes a full email suite. Decide whether you want one bill or two.
  4. Mobile app expectations. Skool, Circle, Mighty Networks and Kajabi all provide apps — but Circle’s branded white-label app is on the custom-priced tier. Verify the app experience during the trial, with a real test member.
  5. Gamification vs control. Skool’s points and leaderboard are built-in but fixed; Circle and Mighty Networks give you structure (spaces, rooms, events) instead of game mechanics. Match the mechanics to your audience, not to the demo.
  6. Integrations you already pay for. Zapier, Make, Stripe, your payment provider — verify each on the trial (and note Skool’s Stripe-only checkout with one plan per community at check time).
  7. Export before you commit. Confirm each platform’s member-data export during the free trial, and keep your own opt-in email list from day one — that list is the asset that survives any platform change.
  8. Affiliate economics, if you plan to promote. Verified on official pages (1 Oct 2026): Skool pays 40% recurring commission; Circle pays $100 one-time + up to 20% recurring by tier. Neither is a reason to pick a platform — but if you earn on referral, the difference is real.

Questions

Is Skool really the cheapest?

Cheapest sticker, yes — $9/month Hobby and $99/month Pro at check time, flat rate. But you will likely add a separate email tool, and customization is minimal. Total cost of ownership is what matters, not the entry price.

What do people actually complain about?

The r/CircleSoPlatform thread we start from compares Circle vs Skool directly: Skool fans like the simplicity and gamification; critics report limited customization, no API, minimal email tooling and support that feels thin outside the “inner circle”. Circle critics focus on price as members grow. Threads are anecdotes — verify on your own trial.

Do these platforms pay referral commissions?

Yes, and the rates are verified on their official pages (1 Oct 2026): Skool 40% recurring commission; Circle $100 one-time + 10–20% recurring depending on volume. Mighty Networks and Kajabi also run partner programs; we did not verify their current rates on an official page at check time, so we list none.

Can I test before paying?

All four offer free trials on their own pages (Skool 14-day, Circle 14-day, Mighty Networks and Kajabi trial periods too). Run one real paid-community flow — onboarding, a post, a live event, a payout — during the trial, with a test member.
