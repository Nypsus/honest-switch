Get paid faster — without the awkward emails.

Late invoices have one boring cause: nobody chased them. Following up is the task that keeps slipping down the list — manual chasing burns hours a month, and the longer an invoice sits, the less likely it is to be paid. Below: the tools that do the chasing (and what they charge), the free routes you probably already pay for, and a system that works.

In brief

  • Chaser: from $233/mo (Compact) — gated by your revenue band.
  • Paidnice: $69/mo up to 150 invoices; $99 up to 300 — adds onto Xero/QuickBooks.
  • Free routes: QuickBooks 3 automatic emails; FreshBooks on all plans; Zoho from its Free plan; Wave needs Pro at $19/mo.
  • Float & Upflow: Float doesn’t chase (its own docs); Upflow and Invoiced publish no price.

A chasing system that works — 5 steps

1 · Switch it onthe reminders you already pay for — QuickBooks, FreshBooks, Zoho — today, at $0 extra.
2 · Build the laddera polite nudge, then +3, +7, +14 days — hardening the tone at each step.
3 · Add teethlate fees or a payment plan for repeat late payers; review AR aging weekly.
  1. Invoice immediately and make paying easy — same-day invoice, due date on the document, payment link included.
  2. Turn on the reminders you already have — QuickBooks, FreshBooks or Zoho Books cover this at no extra cost; switch the setting on today.
  3. Build an escalation ladder — one polite nudge before the due date, then +3, +7 and +14 days after, and harden the tone at each step.
  4. Add teeth for repeat late payers — automated late fees where supported (QuickBooks late fees are not retroactive; Paidnice applies fees per group with a grace period) or a short payment plan.
  5. Review a weekly AR aging report and act on the top three balances — pause work or escalate once an invoice passes 30 days late.

Questions

Does QuickBooks Online chase overdue invoices for me?

Yes — up to three automatic emails, each schedulable 90 days either side of the due date; only invoices emailed after you switch it on, one schedule for all customers.

Do FreshBooks, Zoho Books and Wave include reminders cheaply?

FreshBooks includes them on all plans including Lite; Zoho Books lists them from the Free plan up; Wave needs Pro at $19/mo (or its online payments feature).

What do the paid tools add?

Per-customer schedules, SMS and statements (Paidnice SMS from $0.10/message; Chaser and Upflow include SMS), automated late fees, payment plans and portals — the things built-ins don’t do.

What about Float?

Float is a forecasting tool, not a chaser. Its own documentation says it does not send payment reminders — use it to see cash coming, not to collect it.
